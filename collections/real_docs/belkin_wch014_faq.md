## PRODUCT INFORMATION

### What is the Belkin WCH014?

The Belkin WCH014 is a four-port wall charger that uses advanced Gallium Nitride (GaN) technology and can provide up to 140W of power.

### What is the warranty period of the Belkin WCH014?

The Belkin WCH014 has a 2-year limited warranty.

### Does the Belkin WCH014 come with a Connected Equipment Warranty (CEW)?

The Belkin WCH014 has a CEW of up to $2,500.

Height: 3.74 in (9.50 cm )
Length: 2.96 in (7.52 cm )

## FUNCTIONALITY

### How much power can the Belkin WCH014 provide to my laptop?

The power output of the Belkin WCH014 depends on what USB ports are being used. Below are the maximum available for each port.
Top USB-C port only: 140W maximum
Top USB-C port and USB-A port: 100W maximum
Middle or bottom USB-C port only: 65W maximum
Two USB-C ports in use: Up to 65W maximum each
